The Islamic Golden Age (8th to 13th century) was centered in Baghdad under the Abbasid Caliphate. It was a period of intellectual explosion when scholars of diverse backgrounds gathered to translate and advance the knowledge of the ancient world.

## House of Wisdom
At the heart of Baghdad stood the *House of Wisdom* (Bait al-Hikmah), a grand library and university that birthed crucial discoveries in algebra (Al-Khwarizmi), optics, medicine (Avicenna), and astronomy.
