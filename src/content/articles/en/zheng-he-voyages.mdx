In the early 15th century, Admiral Zheng He led the Ming Dynasty's giant treasure fleets on seven diplomatic voyages across the Indian Ocean, long before European explorers reached Asia.

## The World's Largest Fleet
Zheng He's treasure ships were extraordinarily large, capable of carrying thousands of troops, silk, ceramics, and even live giraffes from Africa. The voyages were not for colonization, but to assert Chinese prestige, trade routes, and diplomatic influence.
