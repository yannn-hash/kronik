On July 20, 1969, humanity achieved one of its greatest milestones when NASA's Apollo 11 mission successfully landed astronauts Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin on the surface of the Moon.

## A Giant Leap
"That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind." This landing marked the climax of the Cold War Space Race, proving the limitless bounds of space technology and human bravery.
