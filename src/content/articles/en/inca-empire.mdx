The Inca Empire was the largest civilization in pre-Columbian America. Centered in Cusco, Peru, they conquered the brutal Andean mountain ranges to form a highly organized empire.

## Machu Picchu and the Inca Road
Without knowing the wheel or alphabetic writing, the Incas built a 40,000-kilometer stone road network across the mountains. One of their greatest legacies is Machu Picchu, a precision stone fortress city hidden above the clouds.
