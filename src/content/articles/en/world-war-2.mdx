World War II (1939–1945) was the deadliest conflict in human history, involving the Axis powers (Germany, Italy, Japan) against the Allies (Britain, Soviet Union, US, and others).

## Tragedy and the Nuclear Age
The war was characterized by rapid *blitzkrieg* invasions, the humanitarian atrocity of the Holocaust, and colossal battles across multiple continents. The war ended after the United States dropped atomic bombs on Hiroshima and Nagasaki, opening the gateway to the Nuclear Age.
