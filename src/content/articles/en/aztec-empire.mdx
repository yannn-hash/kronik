The Aztec Empire began as an alliance of three city-states, with Tenochtitlan serving as the grand capital built on Lake Texcoco. They dominated Mesoamerica in the 15th century until the arrival of Spanish conquistadors.

## Architecture and Chinampa Agriculture
The Aztecs built a remarkably grand floating city. To feed their dense population, they created *chinampas* or artificial floating gardens on the lake, which produced abundant harvests year-round.
