The Shang Dynasty (c. 1600–1046 BCE) is the first historically verified Chinese dynasty. It is highly regarded for its rapid advancements in bronze casting and the creation of one of the earliest writing systems etched on oracle bones.

## Bronze and Oracle Bones
The Shang people were highly skilled at crafting intricate bronze vessels for religious rituals. Additionally, they used turtle shells and animal bones for divination, which serve as the earliest written evidence of ancient Chinese characters.
