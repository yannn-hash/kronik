The Mauryan Empire (322–185 BCE) was the first empire to unify almost the entire Indian subcontinent. Founded by Chandragupta Maurya, it reached its golden age under the leadership of his grandson, Ashoka the Great.

## Pillars of Ashoka and the Spread of Buddhism
Following the bloody Kalinga War, Ashoka renounced violence and embraced Buddhism. He carved edicts of peace and human rights onto giant stone pillars across India and sent emissaries to spread Buddhism throughout Asia.
