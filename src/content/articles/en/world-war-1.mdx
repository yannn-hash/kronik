World War I (1914–1918) was ignited by the assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand of Austria. The conflict triggered a domino effect of complex alliance systems in Europe, plunging the world into a global industrial war.

## Trench Warfare and New Weaponry
The war is remembered for the horrors of trench warfare on the Western Front, where soldiers were trapped in mud under relentless artillery rain. It was the first time the world witnessed the use of tanks, fighter planes, and chemical weapons.
