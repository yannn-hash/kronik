The Indus Valley Civilization (c. 2500 BCE), centered in Mohenjo-Daro and Harappa, was one of the three early civilizations of the Old World, coexisting with Ancient Egypt and Mesopotamia.

## Advanced Urban Planning
Unlike its contemporaries that focused on grand palaces, the Indus Valley prioritized urban planning. They possessed highly advanced underground drainage networks, precise street grids, and water management systems unmatched for millennia.
